About Me

Former CFO & Chief of Strategy · General Counsel · Campus Operations

Thank you for taking the time to learn about my work.

For more than twenty years I have led the parts of an organization that have to work every day: its finances, its buildings, its contracts and the people who keep it running. My career has moved across three industries, and each one taught me something I still use.

Healthcare taught me service under pressure. As a regional vice president of operations for Crothall Healthcare, I directed support-service operations for eleven hospitals with $15 million in departmental budgets. Hospitals do not close, so I learned to run operations that cannot fail and to earn trust from executives who measure results monthly. That work produced $7 million in additional services and a five-year contract extension.

Real estate taught me to build. As a partner, Vice President of Operations and General Counsel at Campus Crest Real Estate Development, I helped grow a start-up to 30 student-housing communities and approximately $1.2 billion in assets at 95 percent occupancy. I directed master planning, capital allocation, construction and acquisitions, negotiated land, financing and rezoning, and managed the company’s legal affairs.

Higher education taught me stewardship. At Central Texas College I served on the Chancellor’s cabinet as Chief of Strategy and then as Chief Financial Officer, accountable for a $102 million budget and, through subordinate leaders, a worldwide workforce exceeding 1,200. I led the college through a SACSCOC review with no findings and through the House Bill 8 performance-funding transition. Today, as Director of Physical Plant at McLennan Community College, I lead about 100 employees, manage $10 million in operating and capital budgets, and am leading the college’s first facilities master plan in about a decade, supported by a campus digital twin I built.

Along the way I have stayed a builder. I write software, including browser-based planning tools and AI training simulations, and I am a founder and partner in two technology companies, EX MULTIS and EGON Safety Technologies. I have also served as an elected board member of the Copperas Cove Economic Development Corporation and an appointed member of the Central Texas Council of Governments Economic Development Board, where I helped bring solar and other renewable energy projects to the region.

What ties it together is how I lead: visibly and collaboratively, with respect for the expertise of the people doing the work, disciplined use of data, and an owner’s mindset toward safety, service and long-term stewardship. My legal and financial training lets me see risk early; my operating experience keeps me grounded in what it takes to deliver.

I would welcome a conversation about how I can help your organization.
