Executive Profile

Senior executive with more than two decades of leadership across finance, operations, strategy and law in higher education, real estate development and healthcare. Former college Chief Financial Officer and Chief of Strategy accountable for a $102M institutional budget and, through subordinate leaders, the majority of a worldwide workforce exceeding 1,200 employees. Currently directs approximately 100 employees and $10M in operating and capital budgets as Director of Physical Plant for a Texas community college, where he is leading the first facilities master plan in about a decade, supported by a campus digital twin and browser-based decision tools he built. Earlier served as partner, Vice President of Operations and General Counsel for a student-housing developer that grew to 30 communities and approximately $1.2B in assets, and directed support-service operations for eleven hospitals as a healthcare-services regional vice president. Founder and partner in two technology ventures.
